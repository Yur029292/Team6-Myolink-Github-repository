# M04

I support our change to an isometric task, pushing the palm up against a table instead of lifting the forearm. When the arm moved, the cables and electrodes shifted and our amplifier got stuck near 3140, so whole recordings were lost. With the arm still, the signal stayed centred and movement was clearly larger than rest. My doubt is that pushing force was not measured, so effort may change between trials and sessions.

Source: De Luca, C. J., Gilmore, L. D., Kuznetsov, M., & Roy, S. H. (2010, May 28). Filtering the surface EMG signal: Movement artifact and baseline noise contamination. Journal of Biomechanics; PubMed. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbiomech.2010.01.027
